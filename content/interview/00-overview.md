**106 questions** across 13 categories, tagged **Easy / Medium / Hard**. Each has: *what the interviewer is testing*, a *structured approach*, a *model answer*, *assumptions and trade-offs*, *common mistakes*, and where useful a *60-second version*. Model answers are examples of good reasoning, **not scripts** — interviewers detect memorised answers. All company scenarios are hypothetical unless stated.

!!! source "From your course notes (Lectures 22, 30, 52, 53)"
    Introduce yourself with relevant evidence (current role and product impact first). Explain your product, users, data flows and your *actual* contribution; distinguish outcomes from tools (story points are not success). **Clarify which metric fell before building a root-cause analysis.** Prepare honest STAR stories; **do not misrepresent your job title, invent metrics or claim work you did not do** — describe course and personal projects as such. Lecture 44 (Interview Prep 4) had no recording and is not summarised.

### A universal answer structure

```chart
type: steps
title: CIRCLES-style structure for product questions (adapt, don't recite)
steps:
  - ["Clarify", "Goal, scope, constraints, definitions"]
  - ["Users", "Segments; pick one with a reason"]
  - ["Needs", "Jobs and pain points (with evidence you'd gather)"]
  - ["Solutions", "3 options incl. a simple one"]
  - ["Prioritise", "Impact, effort, risk; choose"]
  - ["Measure", "Primary metric, guardrails, experiment"]
  - ["Summarise", "Recommendation + risks + next step"]
caption: CIRCLES (popularised by Lewis Lin) is one of several mnemonics. Spend most time on users, needs and trade-offs.
per_row: 4
```

### Mock interview scorecard

| Dimension | 1 · Weak | 3 · Solid | 5 · Excellent | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Structure & clarity | Rambling, no framing | Clear structure, signposting | Crisp, adaptive, summarises well | |
| Clarifying & assumptions | Jumps in | Asks key questions | Prioritises the questions that change the answer; states assumptions | |
| User empathy | Generic users | Specific segment and needs | Deep insight into jobs, pains, contexts | |
| Problem solving & creativity | One obvious idea | Several sensible options | Novel yet feasible; considers non-tech fixes | |
| Prioritisation & trade-offs | No trade-offs | Explicit criteria | Quantified, with risks and sequencing | |
| Metrics & analytics | Vanity metrics | Primary + guardrail | Precise definitions, causal testing, segment thinking | |
| Technical judgement | Hand-wavy | Correct basics | Anticipates failure modes, cost, scale | |
| Communication & presence | Unclear | Clear | Engaging, concise, handles pushback | |
| Integrity | Overclaims | Honest | Transparent about uncertainty and own contribution | |

**Bar guidance (rough):** average ≥ 3.5 with no 1s for a PM offer at most levels; senior roles need 4+ on strategy, trade-offs and leadership.

### Practice plan
Week 1: product sense/design (2/day). Week 2: analytics, RCA, estimation. Week 3: strategy, prioritisation, technical. Week 4: AI PM, behavioural, leadership + 3 full mocks with a partner using the scorecard. Record yourself; review against the rubric.
