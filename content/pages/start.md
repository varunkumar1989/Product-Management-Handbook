## What this handbook is

A visual, structured study system for Product Management, built from **your 55-lecture course study guide** and extended to the **12 Product Core modules**. It is designed for three jobs: learning, applying, and interview preparation.

!!! source "Source integrity"
    - Content marked **"From your course notes"** is preserved from your PDF study guide, with the guide's own corrections applied.
    - Content marked **"Hypothetical example"** uses invented numbers for teaching. It is never presented as real company data.
    - Company examples use only widely known public facts and are labelled as illustrative. Nothing here claims a real company's results.
    - Lectures **44** and **54** had no recordings; this handbook says nothing about their content.
    - The curriculum screenshot did not arrive, so module titles come from your brief and are **provisional**.

## How each topic is taught

Three depths, marked by colour:

* **Level 1 · Beginner** — plain English.
* **Level 2 · Working PM** — how you use it in daily work.
* **Level 3 · Senior PM** — strategy, trade-offs, money and risk.

Around those you will find worked examples, visuals, common mistakes, interview angles, exercises with model answers (hidden until you click), key takeaways and a short quiz.

## Suggested path

1. **Modules 1 → 3**: role, strategy, discovery.
2. **Modules 4 → 7**: framing, UX, communication, analytics (the highest-leverage skills in interviews).
3. **Modules 8 → 9**: growth and technical fluency.
4. **Modules 10 → 12**: AI foundations, building with AI, agentic systems.
5. Throughout: use the **Framework Library**, **Case Studies** and **Interview Handbook**; complete the **Projects** for your portfolio.
