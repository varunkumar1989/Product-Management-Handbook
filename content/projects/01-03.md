!!! hypo "Synthetic data"

### Problem brief
A card issuer's digital application converts only **42%** of starts to submissions and **19%** to activated cards. Leadership asks you to raise submissions by 5 points in two quarters **without** increasing fraud or credit risk.

### Learning objectives
Funnel and segment analysis · onboarding UX for regulated products · prioritisation with risk partners · experiment design · stakeholder communication with risk/compliance.

### Stakeholders
Head of Acquisition (sponsor) · Risk/Credit policy · Compliance/Legal · Fraud operations · KYC vendor manager · Engineering (mobile, web) · Design · Customer support · Marketing.

### Research approach
1. **Quantitative:** funnel by step × device (dataset); time-on-step; error logs at document upload.
2. **Qualitative:** 8 interviews with recent abandoners (consented), 5 usability sessions on mobile web.
3. **Secondary:** support tickets tagged “application”; vendor SDK error reports.

### Analysis of the dataset (model solution)

| Device | Starts | Submitted | Submit / start | Upload pass rate (income → upload) | Activated / start |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| iOS app | 9,000 | 5,000 | 55.6% | 80.0% | 26.9% |
| Android app | 18,000 | 7,601 | 42.2% | 66.0% | 18.8% |
| Mobile web | 15,000 | 4,422 | 29.5% | 52.0% | 12.0% |
| Desktop web | 8,000 | 4,016 | 50.2% | 74.0% | 23.3% |
| **All** | **50,000** | **21,039** | **42.1%** | **66.2%** | **18.9%** |


**Insight:** if mobile web reached Android's upload pass rate (66%), submissions there would rise from ~4,422 to ~5,600 (≈ +1,200/month, ≈ +2.4 points overall), holding later steps constant — half the goal from one fix.

### Templates to use
PRD (Templates library) · Experiment plan · Stakeholder map · Launch checklist · Decision log.

### Step-by-step implementation
1. **Frame:** problem statement and goal with guardrails (fraud, early-delinquency indicators, complaints).
2. **Diagnose:** funnel by device; upload error codes; 5 usability sessions → findings (low light, file size limits, no camera guidance, timeouts).
3. **Ideate & prioritise:** camera guidance/auto-capture, file-size compression, save-and-resume, status tracker, pre-fill (with consent), soft-eligibility messaging (compliance check). Score with weighted criteria incl. *risk* and *compliance effort*.
4. **PRD:** for “Camera guidance + save-and-resume (mobile web)”.
5. **Experiment:** A/B on mobile web; primary = submit/start; secondary = upload pass rate; guardrails = fraud flags, manual-review backlog, complaints.
6. **Launch plan:** staged rollout, support macros, compliance sign-off.
7. **Readout & next steps.**

### Model PRD excerpt
**Problem:** mobile-web applicants fail at document upload (52% pass vs 80% iOS). **Goal:** upload pass ≥ 66% and submit/start ≥ 35% on mobile web within 8 weeks. **Requirements:** live camera guidance (edge detection, glare warning); automatic compression to vendor limits; retry without losing data; save-and-resume link (consented, expires in 7 days, no PII in URL); accessible alternatives (upload from gallery). **Out of scope:** changing KYC policy. **Risks:** fraud via altered images → vendor liveness checks unchanged; data retention → follow policy.

### Experiment plan (model)
* Hypothesis: guidance + compression raises upload pass from 52% to ≥ 62%.
* Unit: applicant session (new applications only). Split 50/50.
* Sample: baseline 52%, MDE +6 pts → ≈ 1,075 per arm at α 0.05, power 80% (computed with Module 7 formula) — ~1 week of mobile-web traffic; run 2 full weeks.
* Guardrails: fraud flag rate, manual-review queue time, complaints.

Funnel analysis (chart + table) · research synthesis (affinity map) · prioritised opportunity list · PRD · experiment plan · stakeholder map · launch checklist · 8-slide readout.

### Review criteria
Correct funnel maths and segmentation · evidence-based problem choice · risk/compliance considered explicitly · testable requirements · powered experiment with guardrails · clear recommendation and trade-offs · honest labelling of synthetic data.

!!! hypo "Synthetic data"

### Problem brief
A food-delivery app wants to raise checkout conversion this quarter. You have event data, an order table, a backlog with rough estimates and budget for one experiment.

### Learning objectives
SQL funnels · customer research · RICE with sensitivity · experiment analysis · readout writing.

### Stakeholders
Growth lead · Checkout engineering · Design · Payments · Analytics · Support · Finance (fees).

### Research approach
SQL funnel (Module 7 queries) → 6 interviews with recent abandoners → review mining → usability test of fee display.

### Step-by-step with model solution
1. **Funnel (dataset):** app_open 792 → view_menu 716 (90.4%) → add_to_cart 423 (59.1%) → checkout_start 322 (76.1%) → payment_success 284 (88.2%). Biggest relative loss: menu → cart; but checkout → payment is closest to revenue and cheaper to fix.
2. **Research synthesis (hypothetical):** fee surprise (6 of 8), payment failure dead ends (4 of 8), slow menus (3 of 8).
3. **RICE (rice_backlog.csv):** fee early 32,000; order-again 28,000; payment retry 14,400; saved addresses 12,500; dark mode 8,000; voice 600. Sensitivity: fee early drops to 20,000 at 50% confidence.
4. **Choose:** run the A/B test on early fee display; schedule payment retry next.
5. **Analyse (ab_test_checkout.csv):** 70.0% vs 72.5% → +2.5 pts, z = 3.91, p ≈ 0.00009, 95% CI [+1.25, +3.75]; complaints 1.18% vs 1.21%.
6. **Readout & decision:** ship with 5% holdout; monitor fee-estimate accuracy.

title: "Project 3 · Improve a collaboration platform's channel-creation UX"
category: "B2B SaaS|UX|Research"
order: 3
summary: Evaluate and redesign a channel-creation workflow using heuristics, usability testing and metrics.
---

!!! hypo "Synthetic data"
    Dataset: `datasets/channel_creation_usability.csv` (8 participants × 3 tasks, synthetic). The product is a generic team-chat tool; any resemblance to a specific product's UI is illustrative.

### Problem brief
Teams create duplicate channels and can't find existing ones; leaders want healthier, more discoverable channel structures.

### Learning objectives
Heuristic evaluation · usability testing · task metrics and SUS · redesign with wireframes · B2B experiment design (workspace-level randomisation).

### Stakeholders
Product lead for collaboration · Design · Engineering · Admin/IT customer advisory board · Customer success · Analytics.

### Research approach
Heuristic evaluation (3 evaluators) → moderated usability test (8 participants, 3 tasks) → analytics (duplicate-channel rate, channel-message share).

### Baseline usability results (model solution from dataset)

| Task | Success | Median time | Errors |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Create a project channel | 6 / 8 | 81.5 s | 5 |
| Find an existing channel | 4 / 8 | 169 s | 9 |
| Set a channel purpose | 5 / 8 | 61 s | 7 |
| **SUS (mean)** | | **62.8** | (below the ~68 average) |

```chart
type: bar
title: Baseline task success (synthetic, n = 8)
cats: ["Create channel", "Find existing channel", "Set purpose"]
series:
  "Participants succeeding": [6, 4, 5]
caption: "Find existing channel" is the weakest task — consistent with duplicate channels.
```

### Step-by-step implementation
1. Heuristic evaluation of current flow (visibility, recognition vs recall, error prevention).
2. Usability test (scripts in Module 5); synthesise findings by severity.
3. Redesign: similar-channel suggestions at creation; naming templates; purpose pre-fill; improved directory search (see wireframe in Case 7).
4. Prototype in Figma; re-test with 6–8 new participants; target ≥ 7/8 on “find existing channel”.
5. Define metrics and A/B at workspace level.

### Model findings and redesign rationale
* *Find existing channel* fails because search ranks by recency, not relevance (heuristic 6: recognition rather than recall) → show similar channels at creation and improve search ranking.
* *Set purpose* errors: field hidden after creation → prompt purpose in the creation dialog with a template.
* SUS 62.8 → target ≥ 70 after redesign.

### Experiment plan
Unit: workspace (to avoid contamination within teams). Primary: duplicate-channel rate. Secondary: channel-message share, search success. Guardrails: channel creation time, admin complaints. Duration: 4 weeks.

### Deliverables
Heuristic report · usability findings with clips/quotes · redesigned wireframes/prototype · metric definitions · experiment plan · stakeholder update.

### Review criteria
Severity-ranked findings tied to evidence · design addresses root causes · accessible design · B2B-appropriate experiment unit · clear success metrics.

### Portfolio guidance
Before/after screens, task-success chart before vs after re-test, three key insights, and the experiment plan. If you completed similar Slack channel-UX work in your own projects folder, link it here as the hands-on artefact.
